# ⌃ convexity

> **Be long convexity. Trade the breakout. Let the stop be the edge.**

---

### Trend is long convexity.
A fat **right** tail — a few breakouts pay for the whole book.

### Short convexity breaks your bank.
Nickels in front of the steamroller. The **left** tail comes for all of it.

---

### Direction is a coin-flip you lose.
Put a stop on it and your hit-rate sits **below 50%** — volatility shakes you out.

### Volatility is an institutional edge.
Not retail's. **Retail has no edge** — not in direction, not in information.

---

### Your only edge is the stop-loss.
Not prediction. Not win-rate. **Risk.**

**Cut losers fast** — small, capped.
**Let winners run** — rare, enormous.

### The strategy is breakout.

```
range ──▶ breakout ──▶ ride the trend
                │
                └─▶ stop ✂  (small, capped)
```

---

*No edge. Sub-50% hits. The convex tail does the work.*
